# Railhead Labs playbooks

Open playbooks for the processes every company runs.

Each playbook explains, in plain language, how a common process runs today and how it runs after redesign with AI. It shows where people stay in charge, which controls you need, and how to build it step by step. Free to read and use, with us or without us.

Browse them on the web at [railheadlabs.com/library](https://railheadlabs.com/library).

## How every playbook is organized

Every playbook follows the same method: **Map → Redesign → Build → Run**.

1. **Who this is for:** the signs that the playbook fits your company.
2. **How it runs today:** the usual steps, handoffs and where the hours go.
3. **Measure your own baseline:** a worksheet to fill in with your own numbers in one week.
4. **The redesigned process:** what AI does, what a person does, and how work is routed by confidence.
5. **What stays with people:** the judgment calls and relationships that people keep.
6. **Controls and risks:** accuracy targets, audit trail, fallback and data handling.
7. **How to build it:** map, redesign, build and run, with a reference architecture.
8. **How to know it is working:** the metrics and how often to review them.
9. **Getting started checklist** and **related playbooks**.

The playbooks contain no benchmark figures. Every number is a labeled example, so you can work out your own.

## A note on regulated processes

Playbooks for financial services, healthcare and security operations touch laws and regulations. They are general information, not legal or compliance advice. Confirm requirements with your counsel and compliance team before you build.
